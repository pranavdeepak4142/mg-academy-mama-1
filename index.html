Beginner Art Course

👶 Age Group: 4 – 8 Years

Learn basic shapes, colouring, and simple drawing. An exciting creative foundation where little artists develop confidence and motor control.

  • ✓ Basic Shapes: Circles, squares, triangles & pattern recognition
  • ✓ Colouring Skills: Neat border-filling, crayon & oil pastel techniques
  • ✓ Simple Drawing: Animals, fruits, houses, flowers & cartoon doodles
  • ✓ Hand-Eye Coordination: Pencil grip and stroke balance

Intermediate Art Course

👦 Age Group: 8 – 12 Years

Learn shading, scenery, painting & creative art. Takes growing students deeper into textures, realistic depths, and colorful landscapes.

  • ✓ Pencil Shading: Tonal gradients, light & shadow, cross-hatching
  • ✓ Scenery Composition: Mountains, sunsets, nature landscapes & perspective
  • ✓ Painting Techniques: Watercolours, poster colours & fluid brushwork
  • ✓ Creative Art: Still-life objects, 3D illusions & imaginative art projects
Enroll in Intermediate Art →
Age: 10 & Above
🖌️

Advanced Art Course

🧑 Age Group: 10 Years & Above

Comprehensive advanced art for older students and aspiring artists — focusing on realistic drawing, canvas paintings, portraits, and portfolio mastery.

  • ✓ Advanced Sketching: Human anatomy, facial features & realistic portraits
  • ✓ Canvas Painting: Acrylics & mixed-media onto stretched canvas
  • ✓ Realistic Rendering: Complex textures (glass, fabric, water, metal)
  • ✓ Creative Mastery: Original concept art, competitions & portfolio showcase
